## About

I'm a software developer focused on **Web3 engineering, smart contracts, and protocol development**.

I build blockchain systems from the ground up — from contract architecture and protocol mechanics to testing infrastructure, integrations, and application layers.

My primary focus is the Ethereum ecosystem and EVM-compatible chains. I enjoy working on complex systems where security, economic incentives, decentralization, and architectural decisions matter.

I also build general-purpose software, developer tools, and backend systems.

**Current interests:**

## Currently Building

### BERT DAO

**Stablecoin-native grant infrastructure**

BERT DAO is my primary project: an on-chain system for coordinating community funding and selecting ideas through stake-backed mechanisms.

The protocol explores how stablecoin commitments, transparent proposal selection, refundable pledges, and milestone-based funding can work together in a decentralized funding system.

**Focus areas:**
- USDC-based funding mechanisms
- Proposal and funding-round lifecycle
- Stake-backed selection and voting
- Refundable pledge accounting
- Treasury and fee management
- Funding eligibility and settlement rules
- Smart contract architecture and testing

**Network:** Arc Testnet

**Status:** Active development. Testnet software; not independently audited.

- [Website](https://bertdao.vercel.app)
- [BERT DAO repository — add the correct URL](https://github.com/Tenyokj/bert-core-arc)

Contributions, technical feedback, and discussions are welcome.

### ICFT Protocol

**Consensus protocol · Distributed systems**

I'm also actively developing ICFT Protocol with a team.

The project focuses on consensus and protocol-level engineering, exploring the mechanisms required for distributed participants to coordinate and reach agreement.

**Current focus:**
